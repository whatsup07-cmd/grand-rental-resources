Bring the event details together

Before asking about an inflatable, have your event date, location, approximate guest ages, and available setup space ready. Tell the rental team whether you are considering a bounce house, water slide, or obstacle course, and ask what is available for that date.

Describe the setup location

Share the surface type, access route, nearby obstacles, and available power. For a water slide, ask about water needs as well. Have the rental provider confirm the specific setup requirements for the unit you choose; requirements can differ between inflatables.

Confirm the arrangements

Ask who handles setup and pickup, what supervision is required, and which weather and operating instructions apply. Request the actual reservation, cancellation, and weather terms before booking. This guide does not set Grand Rental Scottsburg’s rental policies.

Grand Rental Scottsburg also rents equipment for construction, landscaping, and home improvement, including skid steers, excavators, trenchers, and floor sanders. Equipment delivery is available. Call to discuss scheduling and pricing.
